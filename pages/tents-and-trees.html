Tents and Trees

Size:
Tap places:

Put one tent (▲) beside each tree (♣): above, below, left or right. Tents never touch each other, not even diagonally. The numbers show how many tents go in each row and column. Every puzzle has exactly one solution.

Tap a square to place the chosen mark; tap it again to remove it. Tap a row or column number to fill its empty squares with grass. Black squares show a problem.
