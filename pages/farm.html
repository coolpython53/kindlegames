Farm & Mine

How to play

Pick a tool, then tap a square near you (up to 2 away). Tap a tool again to switch what it uses.

Pick digs blocks. Build places blocks. Plant puts seeds on top of dirt or grass. Tap a ripe crop to harvest it.

Crops grow each time you sleep. Sleeping also refills your energy. Mining costs energy; farming is free.

Walk to the $ shop to sell what you gather and buy seeds and better pickaxes. Save up $3000 for the golden trophy.
