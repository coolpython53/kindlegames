How to play. Pick a tower, then tap an empty white square to build it. Tap a tower to see its range. Enemies walk the gray path from > to B. Each press of Next moves the game one turn.

Each turn: bullets fly (*), then enemies step, then towers fire. Bullets start at the tower and take a turn or more to arrive, so aim ahead of the crowd.

Towers. Gun: range 2, 1 damage, fires every turn, bullet moves 1 square a turn. Cannon: range 2, 2 damage to everything within 1 square of impact, fires every 3 turns, bullet moves 2 squares a turn. Sniper: range 5, 3 damage, fires every 3 turns, bullet moves 3 squares a turn.

Enemies. All enemies move 1 square a turn. Numbers show health. T = tank (extra health, costs 2 lives if it reaches the base). Bullets that chase too long (8 turns) fizzle.
